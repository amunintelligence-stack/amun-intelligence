# Google AI Mode / AI Overviews — links move higher, official-sites box, creator profiles clickable

**Date of finding:** 2026-10-17
**Window covered:** last ~30 days

## What changed

- **On 15 Oct 2026 Google announced five link-visibility changes to AI Mode and AI Overviews** (blog post by Hema Budaraju, VP Product Management for Search; promoted by Nick Fox, SVP Knowledge & Information):
  1. **More inline links, placed higher:** "continued and substantial increases" to inline links embedded in the answer text, shown higher in responses more often. Google gave no magnitude, no click figures, no country list.
  2. **Official-sites section (test):** for task-based queries with only one or two official sources, a section at the top lists those official sites with context on why each is useful (Google's example: an airline refund). Test status, no rollout date.
  3. **Dive Deeper redesign:** related-article links at the end of responses get image thumbnails and a more clickable design.
  4. **News carousels expand:** timely-article carousels roll out to more locations and languages, prioritizing the user's **Preferred Sources** (usage up **7x since the start of 2026**); carousels can also pull in relevant social-media posts and forum content.
  5. **Clickable creator/publisher names (test):** on AI Mode video results, creator and publisher names link to their Search profiles (profiles open in Canada, India, US).
- **Also announced in the same post:** Google's News AI pilot licensing program now covers **400+ publications worldwide** (paid delivery/use of enhanced content for new AI features).
- **Why now (context, not Google's claim):** Chartbeat reported news publishers' Google referral traffic fell **~40% between July 2025 and July 2026**. The changes are widely read as a response to publisher pressure.
- **Related trend (Ahrefs analysis, Oct 2026):** AI Overviews appeared on **61.3% of branded searches in July, 65.1% in August, 73.3% in September** (232.8M US desktop SERPs analyzed), reaching **82.9% on 29 Sep**. Wikipedia, YouTube and LinkedIn were among the most-cited external sources inside branded AI Overviews.

## Why it matters for ACI

- **Citation visibility is now a measurable brand-intel signal:** "is this brand cited/linked in AI answers for its own name?" is answerable by observation (AI Mode / AI Overview output for branded queries). ACI's SEO/AI-visibility work should add **AI-answer citation checks** (presence, position, link target, source type) to brand missions — the official-sites box makes "owns the official answer" a distinct, checkable outcome.
- **Preferred Sources is a new ranking lever to track:** 7x growth means a brand's presence on users' Preferred Sources lists increasingly determines whether its reporting surfaces in carousels. For media/brand clients, "are we in the Preferred Sources lists of our audience?" joins the checklist (measured via surveys/panels, not an API — no public endpoint exists).
- **Firsthand-perspective surfacing expands the evidence surface:** Reddit / social / forum content is now surfaced inside AI answers with titles like "Expert Advice" plus creator names/handles. For reputation missions, forum and community mentions are no longer second-order — they can appear inside the AI answer itself.
- **Measurement caveat:** Google published no click-through data for the new placements. ACI must not claim "higher links = more traffic" — measure via Search Console / referral logs where the client grants access, and treat the placement change as visibility-only evidence.
