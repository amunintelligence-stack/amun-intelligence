# New AEO/GEO entrants beyond the Semrush/Ahrefs duopoly: Contentpen 2.0, Somantra, PR Newswire (Sept–Oct 2026)
- Date: 2026-10-11 | Category: data-sources/seo | Action: **WATCH**

## What changed
- **Contentpen 2.0 (launch covered 2026-10-05):** all-in-one SEO/AEO tool tracking brand visibility across 7 AI engines (ChatGPT, Google AI, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Claude) with visibility trends, share of voice, sentiment, source-gap reports, and per-prompt AI optimization. Most relevant: it ships a **public API on every plan** (organization-scoped tokens) for article generation and SEO tooling — i.e., a programmable AEO data source.
- **Somantra AEO Metrics Suite (GA announced 2026-09-29):** three proprietary scores — Brand Mindshare (share of voice + citations), Brand Consideration (positioning vs named competitors), Brand Engagement (multi-turn depth). Claims to track millions of AI search conversations per category. No public API confirmed.
- **PR Newswire AEO & GEO Brand Report (launched 2026-09-28, APAC):** AI-visibility reporting integrated into the PR Newswire Amplify platform, powered by Trajaan (a search-intelligence platform Cision acquired). Mentions / Sources / Answers split — aimed at PR teams, not developers.

## Why it matters for ACI
- Contentpen 2.0 is the most integration-relevant entrant: a public API on every plan is cheaper to evaluate than Semrush's paid add-on or Ahrefs Brand Radar, and 7-engine coverage (incl. Grok and Claude) is broader than the incumbents' lists. But vendor claims are unverified (coverage via third-party press).
- Somantra's metric design (recommendation vs mere mention, multi-turn tracking) is the most analytically mature of the three — worth stealing as a scoring concept for ACI brand-intel even without a partnership.
- PR Newswire's entry signals AEO visibility becoming a PR/comms budget line — relevant for the ACI competitive-intel narrative, not as a data source.
