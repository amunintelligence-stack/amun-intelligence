# AI-visibility tools (Oct 2026) — Ahrefs Brand Radar, Semrush AI Visibility, AI Answer Visibility

**Date:** 2026-10-16 (comparison published 2026-10-14)
**Source:** https://dressx.com/news/the-5-best-ai-visibility-tools-in-2026-ai-answer-visibility-semrush-peec-ai-and-sistrix-compared (secondary — vendor-tinged comparison; treat feature claims as vendor claims, pricing as check-at-purchase)

## What changed
AI-visibility (GEO) tools differ most on platform coverage, how they separate mentions from sources, and whether they keep proof of what an answer looked like on a given day:
- **AI Answer Visibility (More Dash)**: reads an answer across Visibility, Position, Sources, Proof — tracked separately for ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot. Standout: **Proof** — every run stores the full answer, so a brand can open any past result and see what a shopper saw that day. Position tracking shows how early in the answer a brand is named and how it ranks vs. rivals named in the same response.
- **Ahrefs Brand Radar**: brand checked against 448M search-backed prompts across AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, plus Claude for custom prompt tracking. Reports Mentions, Citations, AI Share of Voice, Estimated Impressions; custom prompts addable on top of the pre-built index. AI Visibility Index from $199/mo; Custom Prompts from $50/mo.
- **Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit**: tracks 25 custom prompts on its Base plan ($99/mo), mentions from ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, Perplexity, into one AI Visibility Score benchmarked vs. competitors; includes an AI-readiness site audit tying visibility back to on-page fixes. Enterprise tiers add multi-brand tracking and custom integrations.

## Why it matters for ACI
AI-search visibility is now a first-class intel surface: what LLMs say about a company (mentions, citations, sentiment, share of voice) belongs in a corporate-intel report next to SEO/traffic/ads. The **Proof** pattern — store the full answer per run — is directly adoptable for ACI: any AI-visibility evidence in a mission must carry the verbatim answer + date + platform, not just an aggregate score, so findings are auditable. Ahrefs' 448M-prompt index is the scale benchmark; Semrush's 25-prompt Base plan shows the entry price of the category. All three are paid — ACI's keyless path remains direct prompt-probing (already the Perplexity pattern tracked 2026-10-14), with these as paid upgrades.
