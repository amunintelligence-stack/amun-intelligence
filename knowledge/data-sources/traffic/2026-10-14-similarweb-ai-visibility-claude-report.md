# Similarweb — AI-visibility intelligence and the Claude +540% traffic case study

**Date of finding:** 2026-10-14
**Window covered:** last ~30 days

## What changed

- **Similarweb Market Insights report (late Sep 2026, covered 25 Sep 2026):** `claude.ai` visits grew **+540%**, with paid sources at just **0.46%** of visits in Aug 2026 — direct 73.6%, organic search 12.3%, organic social 5.0%, referrals 4.0%, visits sent by other AI assistants 2.6%, email 2.0% (Jul–Aug 2026 combined). Peer comparison: paid share was 3.5% at chatgpt.com, 6.8% at gemini.google.com, 1.2% at grok.com, 0.57% at perplexity.ai, 0.16% at deepseek.com. Similarweb's summary: chatgpt.com "has started paying for traffic; claude.ai has not needed to."
- **AI-visibility product direction (Oct 2026 listings):** Similarweb AI now markets an **AI SEO Strategy Agent** (keyword-gap analysis, content briefs, offsite opportunities from SERP data), **LLM/AI visibility monitoring** (tracking ChatGPT/Perplexity visibility), a 6B+ keyword SERP index, and mobile/app intelligence. Positioning has shifted from pure traffic estimation to "SEO + GEO (generative-engine optimisation)" intelligence.
- **Accuracy caveat restated (Oct 2026 review):** Similarweb figures remain modelled estimates, most reliable for large high-traffic sites; referral-source data is widely regarded as its most trustworthy output, while organic-vs-direct splits on smaller properties are rough signals.

## Why it matters for ACI

- The Claude case study is a template for ACI traffic reporting: channel-mix decomposition (direct/organic/social/referral/AI-assistant) plus a paid-share benchmark tells a much richer story than a single visits number. ACI traffic briefs should adopt this shape.
- "Visits sent by other AI assistants" (2.6% for Claude) is a new channel category ACI should track explicitly — AI-to-AI referral is now measurable, and it is the earliest signal of a brand's standing inside answer engines.
- Similarweb's pivot to LLM-visibility tracking validates ACI's AI-search data-source work: clients will expect "how often does ChatGPT/Perplexity recommend this company" next to classic traffic numbers. ACI can combine Similarweb-style estimates with its own AI-search probes rather than buying the full enterprise suite.
- The accuracy caveat matters for ACI's confidence scoring: never present Similarweb-style estimates as measured fact; label them modelled, and weight referral data higher than channel splits on small sites.
