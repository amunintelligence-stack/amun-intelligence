# Similarweb free entry points: DigitalRank API (100 credits/mo) and free AI Traffic Checker

**Date found:** 2026-10-19 (nightly dev researcher)
**Action:** WATCH — add the free DigitalRank API (100 data credits/month, global website ranking) to the keyless/free traffic-intel shortlist for low-volume spot checks; verify rate limits and data freshness before any mission use.

## What changed
- Toolsurf's October 2026 Similarweb pricing/guide review (updated ~Oct 1) documents Similarweb's free tier structure:
  - **Free access is limited to public tools:** Website Traffic Checker, AI Traffic Checker, Free Keyword Tool, SERP Seismograph, Free App Analytics, and the browser extension. No free plan for the paid platform; the 7-day trial requires a card, caps at 15 actions/day, and auto-converts.
  - **One API exception: a free DigitalRank API** for global website ranking data, capped at **100 data credits/month** (~1 credit per data point, no-data calls are free, $0.30/credit beyond the cap).
  - Full API access is only on Business/Enterprise plans (via sales) or as a standalone package — entry annual contracts reportedly $15k–$40k.
- The **AI Traffic Checker** (public, free) breaks a site's traffic down by AI platform (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity shares) — a free AI-visibility spot-check surface for any brand.

## Why it matters for ACI
- The DigitalRank API is a genuinely free, documented API path to global website rankings — the cheapest keyless-adjacent traffic-intel source found yet for ACI's traffic connector stubs (alongside the browser extension). Good for low-volume competitive-ranking spot checks in missions; insufficient for bulk work.
- The free AI Traffic Checker gives ACI a no-cost way to answer "how much of this brand's traffic comes from AI platforms?" — a question that otherwise needs paid AI-visibility tooling (Ahrefs Brand Radar $199/mo, Semrush from $99/mo).
- Caveat: free-tier data granularity and freshness are unverified; treat numbers as directional and cross-check with Similarweb/SEM Rush accuracy benchmarks (Owox, Oct 2026: both estimate from sampled panels; small sites = rough estimates).
