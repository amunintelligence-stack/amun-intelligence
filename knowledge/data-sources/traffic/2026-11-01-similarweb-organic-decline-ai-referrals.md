# Traffic — Similarweb/Growth Memo: US organic search −13.2% (Aug 2024 → Aug 2026), AI referrals ~0.5% of visits

**Date noted:** 2026-11-01
**Category:** data-sources / traffic — relevant: SimilarWeb is an ACI traffic data source; AI-referral share calibrates traffic-intel missions

## What changed
- **Growth Memo (Kevin Indig), 26 Oct 2026**, analysing Similarweb monthly traffic estimates for 10,000 domains each in the US and France (Sep 2023 – Aug 2026; 8,658 US and 7,730 French sites with complete organic estimates):
  - US organic search visits **−13.2%** (19.9B → 17.3B); France **−24.4%** (4B → 3B) over Aug 2024 → Aug 2026. Paid search rose 21% (US) / 17.3% (France) in the same window.
  - Publishers lost ~41.5% (US) / ~42.5% (France) of organic visits; ~1% of domains account for 40.2% (US) / 48.5% (France) of gross lost visits — losses are concentrated, not uniform.
  - AI referrals reached **2.85% of organic-search traffic in the US** (2.44% France) — roughly **0.5% of total visits** — and grew fastest of the ten channels tracked, but from a tiny base.
  - Indig's own caveat, preserved here: the data are **modelled estimates, not causes** — they cannot show whether AI or paid search drove the organic losses. The larger French decline is attributed mostly to site selection (Google launched AI Overviews in France in July 2026).
- Cross-checks from the same week: Ahrefs' September 2026 benchmark put AI assistants at **0.43% of all website visits**; SE Ranking's Jan–Apr 2026 panel put it at 0.32%. The 0.43–0.5% range across independent panels is the number to quote, with panel named. The 15 Oct ChatGPT tracking-tag bug (AI visits showing as Direct, fix begun 15 Oct) means October AI-referral figures are likely understated — already covered 2026-10-27.

## Why it matters for ACI
- Traffic-intel missions must stop treating "organic decline" as a single story: the loss is concentrated in publishers and in ~1% of domains. A target company's traffic narrative needs its own vertical cut (publisher vs marketplace vs SaaS), not the headline −13.2%.
- AI referral share (~0.5% of visits) is still a rounding error for most traffic assessments, but it is the fastest-growing channel — missions should report it as a separate line with its panel named, not fold it into "direct" or "organic".
- The causality caveat is a standing rule for ACI reporting: Similarweb modelled estimates describe, they do not explain.

## Action: WATCH
1. Quote AI-referral share as "0.43–0.5% of visits (Ahrefs Sep 2026 / Similarweb via Growth Memo Oct 2026), panel-dependent" in traffic sections.
2. Segment organic-decline claims by vertical; never apply the −13.2% headline to a single company.
