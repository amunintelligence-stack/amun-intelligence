# TikTok US divestiture signing reportedly imminent (Oct 20, 2026) — developing

## What changed
White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt said on Oct 20, 2026 (per BBC, via Marketing-Interactive) that the TikTok US divestiture deal could be signed "in the coming days" — though Beijing has not formally responded. Under the reported framework, a **TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC** majority-owned by American investors (80.1%, incl. Oracle, Silver Lake, MGX) would run US operations; ByteDance retains a 19.9% minority stake and **loses access to US user data and algorithms**, secured on US cloud servers; **Oracle would oversee US data and privacy**, and control of the recommendation algorithm would rest with American entities. Enforcement of the 2024 ban-or-divest law has been delayed repeatedly, most recently to mid-December 2026. **Treat as developing, not finalized:** the deal is announced/pending, not closed.

## Why it matters for ACI
US TikTok is a core social data source for ACI. A change of ownership plus US control of the algorithm will likely reshape US TikTok's data-access policies, Research API terms and ad-library scope over time; the US/global split could also bifurcate datasets (US vs non-US user data). For now, no data-source behavior changes — but the closing (if it happens) is a trigger to re-verify every TikTok integration ACI relies on. Note also the parallel constraint already recorded: Meta's Oct 8 ban on ByteDance/TikTok ads in seven countries incl. the US.
