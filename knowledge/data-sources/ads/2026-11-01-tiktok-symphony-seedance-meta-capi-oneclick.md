# Ads intel — TikTok Symphony Dreamina Seedance 2.0, Meta Conversions API one-click + AI Pixel (week of 31 Oct 2026)

**Date noted:** 2026-11-01
**Category:** data-sources / ads — relevant: Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency, TikTok Creative Center are ACI's keyed ad sources

## What changed
- **TikTok** integrated its **Dreamina Seedance 2.0** AI video model into **TikTok Symphony**: advertisers/creators can now generate complete videos from text instructions or static images without leaving the app (reported 31 Oct 2026). TikTok also obtained a third-party security-infrastructure certification for its US operations in the same update round.
- **Meta** streamlined ad measurement setup: **Conversions API setup is now single-click** (no dedicated engineering support needed), and **Meta Pixel gained AI capabilities** that automatically extract item names, pricing, and page data from business websites.
- No change to the Ad Library / Ads Transparency APIs themselves was reported in this round — the 27–30 Oct Meta Marketing API v26 cliff items (delivery-estimate removals, poll-ads block, Messenger Stories drop, product-browsing default) remain the operative constraints, covered 2026-10-27 through 2026-10-30.

## Why it matters for ACI
- Ad-creative forensics shift: as AI-generated video becomes a one-click default in TikTok Symphony, ACI's ad-intel missions should expect a rising share of synthetic creatives. This pairs with the 25 Oct note on Meta's AI-ad-edits liability (30 Oct): provenance/label detection becomes part of the creative-analysis checklist, not an afterthought.
- The one-click Conversions API lowers the barrier for smaller advertisers to run server-side tracking — expect more complete conversion data in the ecosystem, but also more first-party-tracked campaigns that never appear in public ad libraries. Ad Library coverage stays partial by design; missions must not treat it as exhaustive.
- The Pixel AI extraction (auto item names/pricing) means landing-page data in the ads ecosystem gets richer and more structured — useful for ACI's product/price extraction from ad landing pages.
