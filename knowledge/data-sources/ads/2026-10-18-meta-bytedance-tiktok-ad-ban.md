# Meta bans ByteDance ads and TikTok-destination campaigns in 7 countries (incl. Egypt), effective 8 Oct 2026

**Date found:** 2026-10-18 (nightly dev researcher)
**Action:** WATCH — add a completeness caveat to any Ad Library evidence; don't depend on the library alone for TikTok-destination campaigns in these markets.

## What changed
Bloomberg reported on 8 Oct 2026 (confirmed by a Meta spokesperson to Reuters) that Meta stopped accepting ads from ByteDance, TikTok's parent, on Facebook/Instagram — and the ban extends to third-party advertisers whose campaigns link to TikTok or other ByteDance properties. The seven markets: US, Canada, Egypt, Indonesia, Japan, Thailand, Vietnam. Meta spokesperson Chris Sgro: "We don't have to run ads from a competitor whose goal is to pull people off our apps." Organic posts linking to TikTok are not affected. The move follows TikTok removing Instagram links from profiles in September 2026.

## Why it matters for ACI
Directly degrades Meta Ad Library coverage for TikTok-linked campaigns in Egypt (our operating geography) and six other markets — a category of ads that simply stops appearing in the library, creating a systematic completeness gap for any Ad Library-based competitive-ad intel. Any future Ad Library connector/pipeline must treat absence of TikTok-destination ads in these markets as a policy artifact, not evidence of non-existence, and cross-check TikTok's own Commercial Content Library for those campaigns.
