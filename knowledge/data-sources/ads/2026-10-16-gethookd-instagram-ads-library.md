**Date:** 2026-10-16 (report covered 2026-10-14)
**Source:** https://news.marketersmedia.com/best-free-instagram-ads-library-tools-for-autumn-2026-ranked-in-new-report/89206170 (secondary — press release syndication of a vendor report; treat rankings as vendor marketing, tool facts as usable)

## What changed
A GetHookd report ranks free Instagram ads-library tools for Autumn 2026:
- **GetHookd** itself: analyzes 65M+ Meta ads, with competitor scaling signals, historical creative access, landing-page intelligence, AI-assisted script development and creative variation.
- **Meta Ad Library**: the free baseline — official transparency database, no account needed, active Facebook/Instagram campaigns; limited research depth, no commercial performance indicators, no archive of inactive standard ads.
- **BigSpy**: free tier up to 5 searches/day; broader paid platform covers several ad networks.
- **Minea**: 200-credit free allowance, product-discovery angle for e-commerce.
- **PowerAdSpy**: low-cost paid trial rather than a free plan; broader cross-platform research on higher tiers.
