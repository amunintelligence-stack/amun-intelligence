# n8n 2.45.0 Pre-release (2026-10-20) — Above All Bulletin Floors

## What changed
n8n tagged **2.45.0 on 2026-10-20** on the official releases page (n8n-io/n8n), marked **Pre-release** — i.e. it ships on the beta channel; latest **stable** remains **2.44.4** (2026-10-19). This is the first 2.45.x ever, so no prior coverage exists. Floor check: 2.45.0 > 2.43.5 (Oct-14 stable bulletin floor) and > 2.44.1 (Oct-14 beta bulletin floor) — **PASS, no version regression, no CRITICAL finding**. Notable fixes in the changelog: queue job outcomes now keyed by job on shutdown resume (#40569); parent executions redacted when a sub-workflow uses a credential the viewer cannot use (#41085, #41129); OAuth authorization requests keyed to their own session (#39930); per-request XML parser for webhook bodies (#41098); removed the unused `N8N_SCHEDULER_MIN_INTERVAL` setting; connections reused in the log-streaming webhook destination.

## Why it matters for ACI
Our instance runs 2.43.7 in queue mode and the VM reboots kill n8n ~5–6×/day. The job-outcome keying fix targets exactly the durability hole that matters to us: how queue jobs resolve their outcome when a worker shuts down mid-flight. It pairs with 2.43.7's "suspend in-flight production executions at worker shutdown". The credential-redaction fixes shrink the execution-log leak surface that the Oct-14 bulletin flagged. Do not track 2.45.0 yet — wait for it to graduate from pre-release; it is the upgrade candidate for the next «نفذ» window once stable.
